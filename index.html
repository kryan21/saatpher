Our Philosophy

Feeling first, always.

A wedding is more than how it looked in pictures. We shoot for the feeling in the room — the shaky laugh before the vows, a parent's hand on a shoulder — so years later the photos bring the day back, not just show it.

Rituals

Held close to tradition.

Engagement, haldi, the seven pheras — each ritual as it unfolds, without staging it into something it was not.

Portraits

Yours, not posed.

Portraits that still look like you — relaxed, unforced, on your best day, with nothing stiff about them.

The Edit

Finished with care.

Nothing staged into perfection, nothing invented. Just the real day, edited clean and bright so it lasts.

About Me

The person behind the lens.

Meet Mintu

My photography journey began in 2015, inspired by a passion for creativity that started when I was in 6th standard, experimenting with photo editing on PicsArt.

From designing wedding banners and creating videos to birthday shoots, portraits, weddings, and corporate projects, I have grown step by step through passion, hard work, and experience.

For me, photography is not just about capturing pictures; it is about capturing emotions and creating memories that last forever. My biggest goal is to deliver quality work, build genuine relationships, and earn the trust of every client — trust and client satisfaction will always be more valuable than money.

My belief is simple: do good work, stay honest, and let trust speak for itself.
